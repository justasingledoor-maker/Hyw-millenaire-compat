This file is generated from `src/main/resources/data/hywmill/hywmill_squads/defaults.json`. It lists 16 squads per culture:

**Requirements by quality:**

| Medium | Patron | Guard Post or larger | garrison gear |

**Price:** the members' single prices, less 10%, before the standing discount.

**Cultures without horses or crossbows:** the Maya and the Inuit had no horses, so their four "cavalry" slots are fast foot bands. Japan, India, the Maya and the Inuit rarely fielded crossbows, so their crossbow slots are bow squads.
